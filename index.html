OpenOncology · retrospective benchmark

Which drug would it suggest? Checked against what 2,505 real patients received.

OpenOncology reads a tumor's mutations, copy number changes and gene fusions and ranks the targeted drugs that have evidence for them. We ran it on every patient in Memorial Sloan Kettering's public MSK CHORD cohort (Nature 2024) whose targeted drug matched their tumor under its FDA label, and compared its top three to the drug they were actually given.

What this does and does not show

It measures agreement with what a leading cancer centre prescribed under the FDA label. It does not prove the best treatment for any patient. It is retrospective, on a US cohort, and the ranker does not yet account for line of therapy, which explains most breast cancer misses: patients given a later-line HER2 drug after trastuzumab. An oncologist reviews every result. Not for treatment decisions.
