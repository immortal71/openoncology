# Expansion roadmap

The first offering is treatment-evidence interpretation for sequencing labs. Hospitals without molecular tumor boards are the next segment. These are planned priorities, not delivery commitments or evidence of adoption.

## Initial lab offering

Build a hosted workspace that a lab can evaluate without installing the stack. Fit the report workflow that labs already use and keep evidence sources visible to the reviewing oncologist. The proposed commercial model is $25 per report, with hosted access, support and site validation.

Before deployment, complete and evaluate the application path for relevant alterations across a patient profile. That includes copy-number and fusion integration and the collection of treatment-line information. The [product scope](PRODUCT_SCOPE.md) describes what exists today.

## Local availability and affordability

Add information about whether a drug is available, accessible and affordable where a patient lives. The goal is to put that information beside the clinical evidence. Initial evaluation is planned in Nepal. This data layer is not implemented today.

## Hospital expansion

After evaluation of the lab workflow, offer a hosted workspace to hospitals without molecular tumor boards, with clinical support and validation at each site. Independent oncologist review, a partner-site pilot and prospective evidence are prerequisites for assessing deployment readiness. No completed clinical validation or hospital deployment is claimed.

The [clinical-use roadmap](ROADMAP_TO_CLINICAL_USE.md) records the engineering and study gates. New features and retrospective benchmark results do not close those gates by themselves.

## Longer-term research

Multi-omics interpretation, federated learning and mobile access are research directions without a committed schedule. Their product role will depend on what the initial lab and hospital evaluations show.

The codebase also contains discovery briefs, marketplace and crowdfunding prototypes from the broader research agenda. These are separate from the initial offering. A discovery brief is a research lead; it does not establish a clinically usable medicine, a manufacturing service or a treatment pathway.

## What determines progress

Progress depends on a working evaluation service, reliable evidence reports, independent clinical assessment and partner-site validation. The [project status](../PROJECT_COMPLETION_STATUS.md) records software progress. The [research guide](RESEARCH.md) explains the evidence available so far.
