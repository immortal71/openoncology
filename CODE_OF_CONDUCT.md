# Code of conduct

OpenOncology welcomes contributors from software engineering, oncology and research. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree with their technical or scientific conclusions.
- Give specific, constructive feedback. Explain the evidence behind a claim and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy. Use synthetic examples for support requests and never post patient records, credentials or private correspondence.
- Credit other people's work and follow the licenses of code, data and publications you use.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual attention, personal attacks and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable. Repeated disruption of a discussion after a maintainer asks for it to stop is also unacceptable.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer at [aashish@oncopurpose.com](mailto:aashish@oncopurpose.com). Include the relevant links and enough context to understand what happened. Do not put private or patient information in a public issue.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews reports and may ask for clarification, request a correction, remove content, or restrict participation. The response should reflect the severity of the conduct and whether it is repeated. Reports will be handled privately as far as practical, although information may need to be shared to investigate or address a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents OpenOncology in a public setting.
